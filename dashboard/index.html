How to read this board

Rank is by Implied Value. Implied Value equals Expected Value, which is the size-curve value (what a token would be worth at this TVL, fitted from traded comps) times Launch Odds. P25 is the conservative 25th-percentile estimate. Net EV is the P25 airdrop share for the recommended ticket minus the 90-day farming cost — the P25 value if the token launches.

FARM FARM means P25 Net EV at least $2. THIN THIN means marginal, or check overlap first. SKIP SKIP means 7d outflow worse than −10%, TVL under $8M, or P25 Net EV under −$2. Click a row for Why this rank, the full valuation arithmetic, and the farm plan.

Launch Odds is the fitted probability that a pre-token protocol has launched by the snapshot date, from the snapshot’s own launched-vs-pre-token record: age×TVL cells, adjusted for the category’s launch record and the 7d TVL change. A lower-bound proxy — some pre-token protocols may launch later.
