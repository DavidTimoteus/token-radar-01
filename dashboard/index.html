How to read this board

Rank is by Implied Value. Implied Value equals Expected Value, which is the size-curve value (what a token would be worth at this TVL, fitted from traded comps) times Launch Odds. P25 is the conservative 25th-percentile estimate. Net EV is the P25 airdrop share for the recommended ticket minus the ninety-day farming cost — the P25 value if the token launches.

FARM means P25 Net EV ≥ $2. THIN means marginal, or check overlap first. SKIP means 7d TVL outflow worse than -10%, TVL under $8M, or P25 Net EV under -$2. Click any row for Why this rank, the full valuation arithmetic, and the farm plan.

Launch Odds is the fitted probability that a pre-token protocol has launched a token by the snapshot date, from the snapshot’s own launched versus pre-token record by age and TVL tier (a lower-bound proxy — some pre-token protocols may launch later). Implied Value equals Size-Curve Value times Launch Odds; rank is by Implied Value.
